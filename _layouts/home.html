Next Speaker

Steering Diffusion Models with Bayesian Experimental Design

Dmitry Kangin

The University of Manchester

01 December 2026 | 4:00 PM UTC
Abstract

Although generative models have achieved remarkable quality across domains such as vision and robotics, effectively steering their outputs remains challenging. Users often find it difficult to fully specify their preferences through prompts, while finding it much easier to refine generated outputs through iterative feedback. Moreover, without such feedback, there is no guarantee that generated outputs will faithfully reflect the user’s intent. In this talk, we discuss how techniques such as Bayesian experimental design and Bayesian optimisation can be used to steer (diffusion) generative models using user feedback. We then place these ideas in the broader context of steering generative models for AI for science, where effective user interaction can be particularly important.

About the speaker

Dmitry Kangin is a Research Fellow at the Centre for AI Fundamentals, working with Professor Samuel Kaski. His research focuses on machine learning for science and human‑in‑the‑loop decision making, with particular emphasis on aligning generative models, such as diffusion models, with human goals.
